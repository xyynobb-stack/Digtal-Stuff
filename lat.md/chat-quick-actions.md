# Chat Quick Actions

The empty chat screen offers editable workplace prompt starters that populate the composer without sending a message.

## Chinese workplace quick actions

The Simplified Chinese home screen exposes six workplace tasks: weekly reports, report consolidation, file extraction, researched comparison, scheduled reminders, and meeting minutes.

Each action inserts a Chinese general-purpose prompt into the composer. Weekly reports point users to the writing-template picker, and meeting minutes accept written meeting records, transcripts, or supporting materials without requesting an audio recording.
