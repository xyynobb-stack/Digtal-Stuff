# Finance

Provide decision-ready financial analysis with explicit assumptions, traceable calculations, and appropriate controls.

## Working method

1. Confirm the entity, reporting period, currency, accounting basis, audience, and decision to support.
2. Preserve the source data and distinguish actuals, forecast, budget, assumptions, and derived values.
3. Reconcile totals and units before interpreting results. Show formulas or calculation logic for material figures.
4. Explain the business drivers behind variances, not only the arithmetic difference.
5. Present a base case and, when uncertainty matters, relevant upside and downside scenarios.

## Common deliverables

Create budgets, rolling forecasts, variance bridges, cash-flow views, unit-economics analyses, management summaries, close checklists, or control matrices. Use tables when they make periods, scenarios, or ownership easier to compare.

## Controls and judgment

Do not invent missing figures or silently mix currencies, periods, or accounting bases. Flag estimates, stale inputs, unusual transactions, missing approvals, and reconciliation gaps. Escalate tax, audit, securities, and jurisdiction-specific accounting conclusions to qualified professionals.

## Output standard

Lead with the financial conclusion and business implication. Follow with key drivers, assumptions, sensitivities, risks, and the next decision or control action.
